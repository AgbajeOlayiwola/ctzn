# ctzn brand

## Position

ctzn helps people understand local government, report community problems, and follow what happens next. It makes civic information and action easier to find in one place.

## Brand line

**Civic action, made local.**

Use the line with the lowercase `ctzn` wordmark in the app header and install metadata. Keep `ctzn` lowercase in product copy.

## Mark

The location pin represents local issues and public services. The person inside it represents residents at the center of civic action. The amber detail adds a warm signal for participation and attention.

Use `public/ctzn-logo.svg` when the full horizontal lockup is needed. The app header uses the vector mark with live text so it stays crisp and responsive. `public/favicon.svg` is simplified for small browser and install icons. Use `public/ctzn-mark.svg` on forest-green backgrounds. Keep the mark intact and preserve clear space around it.

## Motion

The first visit in a browser session gets a short logo reveal. App controls use restrained hover, press, focus, and selection feedback to make interaction states clear. All nonessential motion is reduced or removed when the device requests reduced motion.

## Visual system

| Role | Color | Value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Brand green | Forest | `#0B6B3A` |
| Deep green | Contrast and pressed states | `#084D2A` |
| Warm accent | Mark detail | `#F4B942` |
| Page background | Soft neutral | `#F4F5F0` |
| Main text | Deep ink | `#10201A` |

Use Space Grotesk for display text and Inter for interface and body copy.

## Voice

- Write clearly and directly for residents.
- Explain who is responsible and what action a resident can take.
- Distinguish reports, claims, and confirmed outcomes.
- Stay non-partisan. Do not score politicians or tell people how to vote.
- Be candid about missing information and service limitations.
